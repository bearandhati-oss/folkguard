Built for families

Add your parents or grandparents. When their phone catches a scam, you get a quiet alert. They stay independent. You stay in the loop.

The safe word

AI can copy a voice from a few seconds of audio. Your family picks one safe word. If a caller sounds like family and asks for money, ask for it.

Private by design

Every check runs on the iPhone. Messages never leave it. Only the verdict and a one-line reason are shared with your family, through your own iCloud. Read the privacy policy.
